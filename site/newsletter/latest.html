Mississauga pulls nearly 1,900 election signs from illegal spots in two weeks — no candidate has claimed them
Nearly 1,900 signs impounded in less than two weeks — and not a single candidate has come forward to claim them. Election lawn signs, as seen here in Niagara Falls, are in peak bloom across Ontario for the municipal elections. Mississauga has already removed nearly 1,900 illegally-placed signs. Mississauga enforcement officers have removed nearly 1,900 illegal election lawn signs in less than two weeks. In response to an inquiry from Mississauga News, the city shared that between Sept. 27 and Oct. 9, municipal staff have impounded 1,888 non-compliant election signs across the city. The updated total represents a sharp increase from Oct. 5, when staff reported impounding almost 1,300 signs. In 2022, the city impounded approximately 3,500 non-compliant election signs during the entire municipal election cycle, Irene McCutcheon, the city’s senior communication advisor, shared in an email. Candidates began placing election lawn signs on Sept. 27 and by Oct. 5, the city said it had received nearly 120 complaints from residents for illegal sign placement. After removing the signs, the city stores them in municipal work yards for a minimum of 30 days from the date of removal, McCutcheon said.

Toronto's New Emergency Backup Goalie Rule Gives Mississauga's Brendan Bonello Another Shot at the NHL
Insauga.com profiles Brendan Bonello, a Mississauga product now serving as the Toronto Maple Leafs' emergency backup goaltender after the NHL moved to professionalize the role this season. According to the outlet, Bonello played junior and university hockey along with a brief stint in the ECHL before his playing career wound down, after which he began filling in at Leafs practices and game-day skates whenever an extra netminder was needed. The outlet reports the NHL now requires every team to carry an EBUG on its roster, with goaltenders wearing No. 00 who have 80 games or less of professional experience and haven't played under a standard NHL contract or suited up professionally in the past three seasons. Insauga.com quotes the 27-year-old Bonello, who grew up idolizing former Leafs netminder Ed Belfour, saying he doesn't take the second chance for granted. The outlet also cites Calgary's emergency backup Dusty Nickel, a 35-year-old former Junior A goalie who spent five seasons in the role before recently earning a permanent NHL contract.

Mississauga Man, 20, Charged After Unprovoked Attack on Yonge Street Demonstrator
Toronto police say a Mississauga man has been charged with aggravated assault after a demonstrator was punched repeatedly in the head during a weekend protest in downtown Toronto. According to investigators with the Toronto Police hate crime squad, the victim was walking north on Yonge Street near Grosvenor Street and talking with another demonstrator last Saturday when a man approached from behind and struck them several times without provocation. Police say the victim fell to the ground and suffered injuries to their face and leg. The outlet reports a 20-year-old Mississauga man turned himself in at 52 Division on Monday and was scheduled to appear at the Toronto Regional Bail Centre on Tuesday. Toronto Police say the investigation remains ongoing and are asking anyone with information to call investigators or Crime Stoppers.

Trillium Opens Full-Scale Hospital Room Mockups Ahead of New Mississauga Facility's 2034 Debut
Insauga.com reports Trillium Health Partners is giving the public an early look at the future Peter Gilgan Mississauga Hospital through full-scale mockups of hospital rooms at its Trillium HealthWorks Experience Centre. According to the outlet, the new hospital will rise 22 storeys on the same site as the current Mississauga Hospital and is expected to be the largest hospital in Canada when it opens in 2034. The outlet reports the facility will include the Shah Family Hospital for Women and Children, described as the first dedicated medical facility of its kind in Ontario. Insauga.com says Trillium, which also oversees Credit Valley Hospital and the nearby Queensway Health Centre in Etobicoke, is holding open houses on Oct. 20 and Oct. 24 so residents can tour the mockup rooms in person. The experience centre is located just west of Dixie Road at 1225 Dundas St., the outlet reports.
