Ward 5 Councillor Says Year-Old Goreway Bridge Has Ended Decades of Rail-Crossing Gridlock
According to INsauga, Mississauga's Ward 5 Councillor Natalie Hart says the $40-million Goreway Drive Overpass has delivered real benefits to residents, businesses and drivers in the year since it opened. The outlet reports the four-lane structure, built above CN's rail line in Malton near Toronto Pearson Airport, took its first vehicles on October 15 of last year after decades of gridlock along the corridor connecting Mississauga and Brampton. INsauga reports Hart called the overpass incredibly important to her community in several ways, not just for daily commuters. According to the outlet, she said the bridge has improved traffic flow along Goreway Drive as well as other north-south arterial roads in the area. INsauga reports Hart said drivers no longer get stuck waiting at the tracks while trains move goods through the community. According to the outlet, she added that the change has been good for both local businesses and residents.
