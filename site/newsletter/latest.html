CBSA Dog Aggie Flags 16 Kilograms of Undeclared Goat Feet and Ghee at Pearson
According to the Canada Border Services Agency, detector dog Aggie intercepted another large shipment of undeclared food at Toronto Pearson Airport in Mississauga. The agency reports the seizure included 16 kilograms of goat feet, chicken, ghee and milk powder brought in by a group of travellers arriving from Pakistan. CBSA says the travellers were fined $1,350 for failing to declare the items. The agency reports this is the latest in a series of food and animal product seizures publicized at Pearson throughout 2026. According to the CBSA, Canadian law requires travellers to declare all food, plant and animal products entering the country. The agency says undeclared or improperly permitted goods can be confiscated, destroyed or ordered removed from Canada.

Morguard Marks Topping-Off of 431-Unit Rental Project Near Cawthra and Atwater
According to insauga.com, Morguard Corporation held a topping-off ceremony this month for its Boulevard on the Green rental development at 725 Village Green Blvd. in Mississauga, with occupancy expected to begin next year. The outlet reports Brian Athey, Morguard's senior vice president of development, said the fully enclosed building marked the milestone with a ceremonial glass plaque guests were invited to sign. Insauga.com says the project, which broke ground in October 2024 near Cawthra Road and Atwater Avenue, will bring 431 new purpose-built rental units to the city across three mid-rise buildings and townhomes. According to the outlet, the property's zoning allowed for towers up to 12 storeys, but Athey said Morguard chose a lower-rise design to fit the surrounding neighbourhood of single-family homes and townhomes. The outlet reports Morguard has owned the roughly 20-year-vacant site, previously believed to have housed a postal sorting facility, for about two decades.
