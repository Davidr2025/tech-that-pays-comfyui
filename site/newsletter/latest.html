Scam targets drivers who use pay-and-display parking machines across Mississauga
Mississauga officials are again alerting drivers after more bogus QR code stickers were placed on parking machines across the city in attempts to steal people’s personal and financial information. In a public safety notice issued Thursday morning, the City of Mississauga said officials became aware on Wednesday of fraudulent QR code stickers appearing on pay-and-display parking machines at various locations. “City staff were deployed immediately and removed these stickers from parking machines,” the city said in its notice. Officials issued a similar warning to motorists in April. At that time, the fake QR stickers were placed on dozens of parking machines in Mississauga’s downtown core near city hall. In its latest notice to the public, the city said such unauthorized QR code stickers are “designed to redirect users to third-party payment sites that are not affiliated with the city. ” Mississauga officials added that “QR codes have never been a legitimate method of payment at city parking machines. They will never be placed on parking machines or used to process parking payments.

Peel Police Charge Two, Still Searching for Third Suspect in Clarkson Apartment Arson Case
Peel Regional Police say two people have been charged and a third suspect is wanted after two deliberately set fires at a Clarkson apartment building near Lakeshore Road West and Southdown Road caused an estimated $1 million in damage. According to police, the fires were set in a third-floor hallway on Sunday, Sept. 20, and Monday, Sept. 21, and investigators believe occupants of one unit were deliberately targeted both times. Police said no significant injuries were reported, though the second fire triggered a large-scale evacuation that displaced several residents, and 18 people were taken to hospital for smoke inhalation. Police said they identified three people believed to be involved in the incidents. Joshua Pedersen, 28, of no fixed address, was arrested in Hamilton on Oct. 15 and charged with two counts of arson disregarding human life, two counts of mischief over $5,000, break and enter with intent to commit an indictable offence, and possession of break-in instruments. Morgan Murphy, 27, of Burlington, was arrested on Oct. 26 and charged with two counts each of conspiracy to commit an indictable offence and arson disregarding human life. Both were held in custody pending bail hearings.

Massive 176,000-Square-Foot Grocery Store Opens in Mississauga with Global Food as Its Draw
Food World Plus, described as Ontario's largest grocery store, opened to shoppers on Oct. 30 at 50 Matheson Blvd. East in Mississauga. According to the store, its 176,000-square-foot facility is built around global food, offering products and ingredients from the Mediterranean, Latin America, Europe, Asia, Africa, the Middle East and South Asia. The store said it aims to give shoppers in Mississauga's diverse community access to ingredients they might otherwise need to visit several specialty grocers to find. Beyond its international aisles, Food World Plus is positioning itself as a one-stop destination with a flower section, bakery, deli, fresh meat department and in-house butcher. The store said its meat department includes a dedicated halal section, with meat sourced from a certified halal supplier and handled according to halal standards from delivery through to the cuts prepared for customers. Mayor-elect Alvin Tedjo attended the grand opening alongside Ontario Premier Doug Ford and later welcomed the new store to the city in a social media post.
