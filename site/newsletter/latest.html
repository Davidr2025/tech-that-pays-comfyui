Don Valley Parkway Reopens After Wednesday's Storm Floods Toronto and Mississauga Streets
According to INsauga, heavy rain from Wednesday's severe storm shut down roads and highways across the Greater Toronto Area, including parts of Yonge Street and the Don Valley Parkway. The outlet reports the storm brought torrential rain, large hail and the risk of a tornado to wide swaths of southern Ontario, darkening skies over downtown Toronto and pummelling neighbourhoods farther north with hail. INsauga reports the flooding also suspended subway service during Wednesday's afternoon rush hour, and Toronto police said crews spent Thursday morning clearing debris from the Don Valley Parkway before its southbound lanes reopened. According to the outlet, Credit Valley Conservation issued a flood warning Wednesday evening after the upper watershed received about 70 millimetres of rain, with flooding also reported in Orangeville's First Street, Second Avenue and Rotary Park areas. INsauga reports Peel Regional Police said Bromsgrove Road in Mississauga also flooded, affecting vehicles on the road as well.

Mississauga Man's Death Seven Months After Being Struck Leads to Upgraded Charge for Tow Truck Driver
According to INsauga, Peel Regional Police have upgraded the charge against a Mississauga tow truck driver after the pedestrian he struck last December died in hospital in late July. The outlet reports the collision happened on Tuesday, December 16, 2025, in the city's east end, when the tow truck struck a 45-year-old man who was rushed to a local trauma centre with life-threatening injuries. INsauga reports the man succumbed to his injuries on July 29, roughly seven months after the crash. According to the outlet, Abner Zuniga, 49, of Mississauga, was originally charged in March with dangerous operation of a vehicle causing bodily harm, and that charge has now been upgraded to dangerous operation of a vehicle causing death. INsauga reports the investigation remains ongoing, with Peel Regional Police's Major Collision Bureau asking anyone with information to come forward.

Snowbirds' Final Labour Day Flyover Headlines 77th Canadian International Air Show
According to INsauga, skies over Mississauga and Toronto will again fill with military and civilian aircraft in the coming days as the Canadian International Air Show returns to the CNE grounds over Labour Day weekend. The outlet reports Toronto Pearson Airport and, to a lesser extent, Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport are serving as home bases for the aircraft taking part, with planes flying over Mississauga and Toronto as they transport to the show site. INsauga reports this year's lineup includes the Canadian Forces Snowbirds, who are pausing operations after 2026, along with Canada's CF-18 demonstration team and an RCMP Black Hawk helicopter. According to the outlet, the show is in its 77th year and is billed by organizers as both Canada's largest and longest-running air show and North America's longest-running air show. INsauga reports the aircraft will be based at Pearson and Billy Bishop through Monday, meaning residents may spot fighter jets tuning up in the airspace over the city in the lead-up to the main event.

Rare Topps Baseball Card Puts Mississauga's Name Ahead of Josh Naylor's Own
According to INsauga, Seattle Mariners first baseman Josh Naylor has been given the nickname Mississauga Masher on a rare variation card in the 2026 Topps Heritage set. The outlet reports the design, which pays tribute to the classic 1977 Topps look, replaces Naylor's name with the nickname, a rare touch for a city with little history of appearing on major-league baseball cards. INsauga reports the card is one of only 10 Nickname Variations included in the set, alongside players such as Bobby Witt Jr., nicknamed Bobby Baseball, Kyle Schwarber as Schwarbs, Cal Raleigh as Big Dumper and Roman Anthony as Roman Empire. According to the outlet, Naylor's card is numbered 129 in the set and is a short print, meaning it is considerably harder for collectors to find than the standard version. INsauga reports the card adds a bit of novelty for a city not typically associated with major-league baseball memorabilia.

Peel Region Cities Rank Among Canada's Best for Investment After Adding Over 5,000 Jobs
According to INsauga, a new report credits major investment projects with creating more than 5,000 new jobs across Mississauga and Brampton over the past year. The outlet reports the figure includes nearly 4,000 new jobs in Mississauga and 1,265 in Brampton, with both cities again ranking highly on Site Selection magazine's annual list of the best Canadian municipalities for investment. INsauga reports notable Mississauga projects included a corporate headquarters expansion worth $127 million that added 136 jobs, along with a $13 million Citigroup expansion that created 600 jobs. According to the outlet, Brampton's gains came from projects including the $141 million expansion of the Coca-Cola Canada Bottling Plant, a new Mobile Climate Control manufacturing and research facility adding 700 jobs, and a fourth advanced manufacturing facility from Powersmiths adding 200 jobs. INsauga reports Ontario ranked first among Canadian provinces for investment in the report, ahead of Alberta and Saskatchewan, with the report crediting the province's business case as remaining strong despite trade friction with the United States.
