A quiet lakeside nature reserve in Clarkson with boardwalk trails through marsh, woodland and beach habitat. One of the last natural shoreline marshes on Lake Ontario, great for birdwatching and an easy, flat walk any time of year.

Free · Park

Take in the aviation history at Paul Coffey Park

Paul Coffey Park, Malton

A large community park in Malton built around an adventure playground with a fairytale theme, complete with castles, turrets and dragon slides, plus a splash pad, trails and picnic areas. A retired Avro Canada CF-100 Canuck fighter jet sits on display near the park, a nod to Malton's roots as a hub of Canadian aircraft manufacturing.

Lakefront Promenade Park, Lakeview

A 40-hectare waterfront park in Lakeview with an unsupervised beach, a boardwalk, a splash pad, beach volleyball courts and a marina with slips for visiting boats. One of the most complete free lakefront destinations in the city, with trails and picnic spots along the shore.

Escape the weather at Hazel McCallion Central Library

Hazel McCallion Central Library, City Centre

The city's flagship library in City Centre, reopened after a major renovation with a fourth-floor Sky Reading Lounge enclosed in glass with panoramic views over Celebration Square. Adamson Estate, Lakeview

A 13-acre waterfront property in Lakeview built around a 1920s manor house, gatehouse and barn, with grounds that are now a public park along the Waterfront Trail. Free parking and a small beach make it an easy stop for a lakeside walk or a picnic among the historic buildings.

Free · Garden

Stroll the woodlot and gardens at Cawthra Park

Cawthra Park, near Cawthra Road and the QEW

Once the private grounds of the 1926 Cawthra-Elliot family estate near Cawthra Road and the QEW, this property was purchased by the City of Mississauga in 1974 and opened for passive park use. Its surrounding woodlot is provincially recognized habitat for the endangered Jefferson salamander, making it one of the city's more ecologically significant green spaces.

The civic heart of City Centre hosts free concerts, movie nights and cultural festivals through the summer, with the reflecting pool converted into an outdoor skating rink each winter. Free · Waterfront

Watch the boats from J.C. Saddington Park

J.C. Saddington Park, Port Credit

This waterfront park at the mouth of the Credit River was built on land reclaimed from a former landfill during 1970s waterfront planning. Looped paths, picnic areas, a playground and an informal amphitheatre sit within view of the red and white Port Credit Lighthouse, one of the most photographed structures in the city.

City of Mississauga Details →
Free · Heritage

Walk the streets of Meadowvale Village, Ontario's first heritage district

Meadowvale Village, Old Derry Road at Second Line West

Designated in 1980, this was Ontario's first Heritage Conservation District, preserving a 19th century mill village along Old Derry Road and Second Line West by the Credit River. Dozens of historic homes and buildings remain largely unaltered, with heights rarely exceeding two storeys, making a stroll through the village core feel like stepping back into Mississauga's pioneer era.

Walk the Sawmill Valley Trail through Erin Mills

Sawmill Valley Trail, Erin Mills

A multi-use trail following Sawmill Creek through a wooded ravine alongside the University of Toronto Mississauga campus, linking up with the broader Credit River trail network near Erindale Park. A mix of paved path, boardwalk and dirt trail makes it popular for walking, running and birdwatching.

Free · Heritage

Visit the Dixie Union Chapel and Cemetery

Dixie Union Chapel and Cemetery, Dundas Street East

A stone chapel built in 1837 by early Toronto Township settlers to replace a shared 1816 log church used by Anglican, Methodist and Presbyterian congregations. Designated a historic site in 1974 as one of Ontario's oldest union chapels, it still stands beside a public cemetery with burials dating back to 1810.

Free · Trail

Fish, walk or cool off at Lake Aquitaine Park

Lake Aquitaine Park, Meadowvale

A paved, wheelchair-accessible trail circles this human-made stormwater lake near Derry Road in Meadowvale, with an outdoor fitness circuit, a fishing dock stocked with bass, perch and crappie, and a spray pad for summer. The lake feeds into the Credit River, and the park stays open from dawn until 11 p.m.

Walk the new river valley trails at Credit Meadows Park

Credit Meadows Park, Churchill Meadows

Newly built multi-use trails link a parking area off Creditview Road to the former Harris Lands by way of two pedestrian bridges over the Credit River, with the first phase completed in July 2025. A quieter, newer stretch of river valley trail on the city's west side, through Churchill Meadows.
