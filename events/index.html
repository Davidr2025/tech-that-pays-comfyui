The Dinner Detective Mississauga: Interactive Murder Mystery Dinner Show

2:00 p.m. · Four Points by Sheraton &#038; Element by Westin Toronto Airport

2nd annual Mississauga Moon and Lantern Festival

1:00 p.m. · Square One Shopping Centre, Lot P6

Join us in celebrating the 2nd Annual Mississauga Moon & Lantern Festival 2026 , a vibrant community event honouring the traditional Mid-Autumn Festival , one of the most treasured cultural celebrations across many Asian communities. As part of the Ontario Asian Night Market Series 2026 , this seasonal festival brings the spirit of the harvest moon to Mississauga through captivating lantern displays, cultural performances, delicious mooncakes, diverse food vendors, unique shopping experiences, and engaging activities for all ages.

The Egyptian Coptic Festival

8:00 a.m. · Mississauga Celebration Square

Celebrate the rich heritage of the Coptic community through a vibrant gathering filled with history, flavour, and community spirit. This festival offers a unique opportunity to explore one of the world’s oldest cultures in a welcoming and lively setting. Enjoy authentic Egyptian cuisine, traditional music, and cultural performances that showcase centuries of heritage. Browse artisan vendors, discover handcrafted goods, and take part in activities for all ages.

Movie Night in The Square: Karate Kid

4:00 p.m. · streetsville village square

Spend an evening in Streetsville enjoying a movie under the stars with a screening of The Karate Kid (2010) . Gather your family and friends, bring along blankets or lawn chairs, and get comfortable in the Village Square for a relaxed and entertaining night out. The movie will begin at dusk, with plenty of pre-movie activities to enjoy—so be sure to arrive early. Free · Nature

Walk the boardwalk at Rattray Marsh Conservation Area

Rattray Marsh Conservation Area, Clarkson

A quiet lakeside nature reserve in Clarkson with boardwalk trails through marsh, woodland and beach habitat. One of the last natural shoreline marshes on Lake Ontario, great for birdwatching and an easy, flat walk any time of year.

Free · Waterfront

Picnic and lakefront views at Jack Darling Memorial Park

Jack Darling Memorial Park, Lorne Park

A large waterfront park with open lawns, a splash pad, off-leash dog area, and direct access to the Waterfront Trail. Bring a picnic and watch the sailboats out of Lorne Park Yacht Club.

Free · Waterfront

Cycle or stroll the Waterfront Trail through Port Credit

Port Credit waterfront

This stretch of the Lake Ontario Waterfront Trail runs past the Port Credit Lighthouse, marinas and Rotary Park, one of the most scenic free walks or bike rides in the city.

Find calm at Kariya Park

Kariya Park, City Centre

A small Japanese garden in the heart of City Centre, with a koi pond, pagoda and manicured paths. A peaceful, free escape just steps from Square One.

Hike the Credit River trails at Riverwood

Riverwood Conservancy, Mississauga Road

Riverwood's trail network winds through forest and meadow along the Credit River, with historic buildings and gardens on site. A favourite for longer nature walks and trail runs.

Explore Erindale Park's river valley trails

Erindale Park, Mississauga Road

One of the city's largest parks, with wooded trails, picnic areas and river access along the Credit River. Good for a longer walk, a run, or an afternoon by the water.

Wander the Culham Trail through Streetsville

Culham Trail, Streetsville

A shaded multi-use trail following the Credit River through historic Streetsville, connecting parks, the village core and quiet residential streets. Easy, flat, and free.

Free · Waterfront

Explore the newly opened Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area

Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area, Lakeview

Built on the former site of the coal fired Lakeview Generating Station and named for the late Mississauga councillor who championed its restoration, this new waterfront conservation area adds several kilometres of trail and boardwalk along Lake Ontario, with restored wetland and meadow habitat. Reached via Marie Curtis Park on the Waterfront Trail.

Walk the Glassford Trail at Meadowvale Conservation Area

Meadowvale Conservation Area, Meadowvale

Home to Credit Valley Conservation's head office, this park follows the Credit River through floodplain forest and open meadow, with picnic areas and gazebos along the way. A quieter, less travelled stretch of the river valley than the trails further south.

Free · Trail

Follow the Cooksville Creek Trail

Cooksville Creek Trail, Cooksville

A paved, easy multi use path through Cooksville's green corridor, linking Richard Jones Park and Mississauga Valley Park before continuing south toward Lakeshore Road and the Waterfront Trail. A good flat route for walking, jogging or an easy bike ride.

City of Mississauga Details →
Free · Park

Take in the aviation history at Paul Coffey Park

Paul Coffey Park, Malton

A large community park in Malton built around an adventure playground with a fairytale theme, complete with castles, turrets and dragon slides, plus a splash pad, trails and picnic areas. A retired Avro Canada CF-100 Canuck fighter jet sits on display near the park, a nod to Malton's roots as a hub of Canadian aircraft manufacturing.

City of Mississauga Details →
Free · Waterfront

Spend a day at Lakefront Promenade Park

Lakefront Promenade Park, Lakeview

A 40-hectare waterfront park in Lakeview with an unsupervised beach, a boardwalk, a splash pad, beach volleyball courts and a marina with slips for visiting boats. One of the most complete free lakefront destinations in the city, with trails and picnic spots along the shore.

City of Mississauga Details →
Free · Indoor

Escape the weather at Hazel McCallion Central Library

Hazel McCallion Central Library, City Centre

The city's flagship library in City Centre, reopened after a major renovation with a fourth-floor Sky Reading Lounge enclosed in glass with panoramic views over Celebration Square. Admission is free, and the building also has a maker space, a gaming lounge and quiet study areas, a solid rainy-day alternative to the city's outdoor spots.
