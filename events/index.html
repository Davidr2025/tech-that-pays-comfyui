Rad Ride & Shine 2026

6:30 a.m. · Huron Park

Rad Ride & Shine 2026 is hitting Mississauga! Aug 21

Mississauga Italfest 2026

1:00 p.m. · Mississauga Celebration Square

Mississauga Italfest is a vibrant celebration of Italian culture in Mississauga. Recognized as one of Ontario’s Top 100 Festivals (2016–2025), the festival takes place each August in celebration of Ferragosto, Italy’s national holiday. The festival brings the community together for a lively weekend filled with live music, traditional and contemporary performances, and authentic Italian cuisine—from fresh pasta and pizza to espresso and cannoli.

Free · Nature

Walk the boardwalk at Rattray Marsh Conservation Area

Rattray Marsh Conservation Area, Clarkson

A quiet lakeside nature reserve in Clarkson with boardwalk trails through marsh, woodland and beach habitat. One of the last natural shoreline marshes on Lake Ontario, great for birdwatching and an easy, flat walk any time of year.

Picnic and lakefront views at Jack Darling Memorial Park

Jack Darling Memorial Park, Lorne Park

A large waterfront park with open lawns, a splash pad, off-leash dog area, and direct access to the Waterfront Trail. Bring a picnic and watch the sailboats out of Lorne Park Yacht Club.

Port Credit waterfront

Erindale Park, Mississauga Road

Wander the Culham Trail through Streetsville

Culham Trail, Streetsville

A shaded multi-use trail following the Credit River through historic Streetsville, connecting parks, the village core and quiet residential streets. Easy, flat, and free.

Free · Waterfront

Explore the newly opened Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area

Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area, Lakeview

Built on the former site of the coal fired Lakeview Generating Station and named for the late Mississauga councillor who championed its restoration, this new waterfront conservation area adds several kilometres of trail and boardwalk along Lake Ontario, with restored wetland and meadow habitat. Reached via Marie Curtis Park on the Waterfront Trail.

Walk the Glassford Trail at Meadowvale Conservation Area

Meadowvale Conservation Area, Meadowvale

Home to Credit Valley Conservation's head office, this park follows the Credit River through floodplain forest and open meadow, with picnic areas and gazebos along the way. A quieter, less travelled stretch of the river valley than the trails further south.

Free · Trail

Follow the Cooksville Creek Trail

Cooksville Creek Trail, Cooksville

A paved, easy multi use path through Cooksville's green corridor, linking Richard Jones Park and Mississauga Valley Park before continuing south toward Lakeshore Road and the Waterfront Trail. A good flat route for walking, jogging or an easy bike ride.
