Privacy Policy

Last updated July 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Mississauga Insider ("we", "us") collects when you visit mississaugainsider.ca, why we collect it, and how it's used. We try to keep this simple and collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, through two forms on the site:

  • Newsletter signup: your email address, submitted through the subscribe form on the homepage, the sticky bar, or https://mississauga-insider-newsletter.beehiiv.com/. This is handled by our newsletter provider, Beehiiv, and used only to send you the Mississauga Insider newsletter.
  • Contact form: the Contact Us page uses an embedded form (powered by GoHighLevel) that may ask for your name, email, phone number, and message. If you check the optional consent boxes on that form, you're also agreeing to receive text messages from us at the number provided. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not use our own tracking cookies or analytics software. The only thing the site stores in your browser is a single, non-identifying preference (whether you've dismissed the sticky newsletter bar), kept in your browser's local storage and never sent back to us.

How we use your information

  • To send the newsletter you signed up for.
  • To respond to questions, story tips, or advertising inquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To send text updates, but only if you opted in on the contact form.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Third-party services

Parts of the site rely on outside services, each with its own privacy policy governing the information it handles:

  • Beehiiv (newsletter delivery): beehiiv.com/privacy
  • GoHighLevel (contact form and messaging): gohighlevel.com/privacy-policy
  • Google Maps Platform (business listing data and photos in our directory): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Some Business Spotlight articles embed a YouTube video or Instagram post. Those embeds are subject to YouTube's and Instagram's own privacy policies when you interact with them.

Our business directory

Listings in the directory are compiled from Google's public business data (Google Maps Platform), not submitted by site visitors. If you're a business owner and want your listing corrected or removed, contact us.

Children's privacy

Mississauga Insider is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time by contacting us. To stop receiving the newsletter, use the unsubscribe link in any issue. To stop text messages, reply STOP to any message.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Contact us.
