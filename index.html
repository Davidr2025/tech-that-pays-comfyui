Sep 29

Henna Workshop

8:30 a.m. · Small Arms Inspection Building

Discover the art of henna in this engaging, hands-on workshop led by artist Ankita Sherkhane. Participants will explore the rich cultural traditions and history of henna through a brief introductory presentation before learning the techniques behind creating their own unique designs. Using individual henna cones, attendees will have the opportunity to apply their creations to themselves or a partner and leave with a beautiful temporary henna tattoo (mehndi) that can be enjoyed for weeks afterward.

2026 All Nations Powwow

7:00 a.m. · University of Toronto Mississauga Recreation, Athletics and Wellness Centre (RAWC)

A Powwow is an Indigenous social celebration where community members can gather and enjoy traditional singing and dancing. All community members, including non-Indigenous, are welcome and strongly encouraged to attend and participate in what a Powwow has to offer. Experiencing a Powwow emphasizes the importance of relationship-building with everyone while providing opportunities to learn more about Indigenous culture and knowledge.
