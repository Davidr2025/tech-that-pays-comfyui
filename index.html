Henna Workshop

8:30 a.m. · Small Arms Inspection Building

Discover the art of henna in this engaging, hands-on workshop led by artist Ankita Sherkhane. Participants will explore the rich cultural traditions and history of henna through a brief introductory presentation before learning the techniques behind creating their own unique designs. Using individual henna cones, attendees will have the opportunity to apply their creations to themselves or a partner and leave with a beautiful temporary henna tattoo (mehndi) that can be enjoyed for weeks afterward.
