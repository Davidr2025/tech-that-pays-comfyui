Mississauga Italfest 2026

1:00 p.m. · Mississauga Celebration Square

Mississauga Italfest is a vibrant celebration of Italian culture in Mississauga. Recognized as one of Ontario’s Top 100 Festivals (2016–2025), the festival takes place each August in celebration of Ferragosto, Italy’s national holiday. The festival brings the community together for a lively weekend filled with live music, traditional and contemporary performances, and authentic Italian cuisine—from fresh pasta and pizza to espresso and cannoli.

Walk the boardwalk at Rattray Marsh Conservation Area

Rattray Marsh Conservation Area, Clarkson

A quiet lakeside nature reserve in Clarkson with boardwalk trails through marsh, woodland and beach habitat. One of the last natural shoreline marshes on Lake Ontario, great for birdwatching and an easy, flat walk any time of year.
