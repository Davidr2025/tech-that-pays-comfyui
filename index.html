2026 All Nations Powwow

7:00 a.m. · University of Toronto Mississauga Recreation, Athletics and Wellness Centre (RAWC)

A Powwow is an Indigenous social celebration where community members can gather and enjoy traditional singing and dancing. All community members, including non-Indigenous, are welcome and strongly encouraged to attend and participate in what a Powwow has to offer. Experiencing a Powwow emphasizes the importance of relationship-building with everyone while providing opportunities to learn more about Indigenous culture and knowledge.

A quiet lakeside nature reserve in Clarkson with boardwalk trails through marsh, woodland and beach habitat. One of the last natural shoreline marshes on Lake Ontario, great for birdwatching and an easy, flat walk any time of year.
