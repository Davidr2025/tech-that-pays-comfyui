Illuminating the Stories of WWII: Movie Screening and Sculpture Talk

2:00 p.m. · Small Arms Inspection Building

This Remembrance Day celebrate and reflect on the history, sacrifices and personal stories of Canadian soldiers and of Dutch civilians during the liberation of the Netherlands in World War II. Come enjoy this multifaceted evening, which includes a photo display, film screening and informative talk. Five photographs by Jason Van Bruggen from the 80 Years of Freedom Exhibition will be on view during this event. There will also be a screening of the film 80 Faces of Liberation also by Jason van Bruggen.

Walk the boardwalk at Rattray Marsh Conservation Area

Rattray Marsh Conservation Area, Clarkson

A quiet lakeside nature reserve in Clarkson with boardwalk trails through marsh, woodland and beach habitat. One of the last natural shoreline marshes on Lake Ontario, great for birdwatching and an easy, flat walk any time of year.
