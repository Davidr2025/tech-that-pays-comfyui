The Art of Presence: Forest Bathing and Sound Immersion

2:00 p.m. · Small Arms Inspection Building

There is an art to slowing down, and most of us have forgotten it. Come be inspired by facilitator Tina De Luca in a two-hour immersive wellness experience inspired by the Japanese practice of Shinrin-yoku (forest bathing). The evening begins outdoors with a guided forest bathing experience around the grounds of the Small Arms Inspection Building. Through mindful walking and sensory awareness, you'll be invited to slow down, notice the natural environment, and reconnect with the present moment.
